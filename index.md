## About me

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of English Linguistics at the University of Stuttgart. My doctoral dissertation is being advised by [Judith Tonhauser](https://judith-tonhauser.github.io/).

My research interests lie in the areas of semantics, pragmatics and gesture studies, but I am also interested in psycholinguistics and sociolinguistics. I focus on phenomenas such as at-issueness, projection, iconicity and multimodal communication.

I study what kind of content iconic gestures contribute in multimodal communication and how these gestures interact with co-occurring speech. To do this, I conduct experiments in English within the project ["Iconic of co-speech gestures in the taxonomy of not-at-issue content"](https://vicom.info/project/iconic-content-of-co-speech-gestures-in-the-taxonomy-of-not-at-issue-content/). This project is part of the DFG-funded Priority Program Visual Communication (SPP 2392 ViCom).
